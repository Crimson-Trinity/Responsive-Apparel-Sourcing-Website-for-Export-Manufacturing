Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us as your sourcing partner.

Do you support private label and custom development? +
Yes. We specialize in private label manufacturing including custom patterns, fabric sourcing, labeling, packaging, and finishing as per the requirements.
What fabrics can you source and manufacture with? +
We work with cotton, cotton blended, recycled, polyester, French terry, fleece, rib, interlock, and performance fabrics.
What are your MOQ and monthly production capacity? +
Our typical MOQ starts from 300–500 pieces per style depending on complexity. Our production capacity exceeds 100,000 units per month.
What is your standard lead time for bulk production? +
Standard lead time ranges from 60-75 days after sample approval, depending on order size and fabric availability.
Do you have certified factories? +
Yes. We work with SEDEX compliant, and SA8000 certified factories.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes. We export to Israel, Dubai and Mexico with FOB and CIF terms.
